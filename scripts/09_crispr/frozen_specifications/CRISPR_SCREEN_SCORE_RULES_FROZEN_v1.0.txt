IAV-P01 — LI ET AL. 2020
============================================================

Workbook:
IAV-P01_Li2020_Supplementary_Data_1_ORIGINAL.xlsx

Sheet:
Secondary screen

Genome-wide evaluable genes:
18,291

Primary ranking variable:
screen p

Supporting robustness variable:
screen remainder p (leaving out strongest sgRNA)

Rationale:
Both variables are available genome-wide.

The validation p-value is available for only 1,737 genes.
Combined statistics are available for only 874 genes.

Therefore:
validation p, combined p, combined remainder p, and combined FDR
MUST NOT be used as genome-wide ranking variables.

They may be used only for secondary targeted-validation evidence.

Primary-screen 41-gene table:
Used as study-defined significant hit list.

INTERPRETATION:
Enrichment among low-HA/infection-resistant perturbations supports
proviral host-dependency function.

============================================================
RANK-BASED TESTING
============================================================

Gene-set enrichment will use complete genome-wide rankings wherever
available.

Empirical permutation will compare observed frozen-set rank statistics
against random gene sets of identical size drawn from the evaluable
screen universe.

Primary rank summary:
mean normalized rank / rank-based enrichment statistic.

Additional sensitivity summaries may include:
median normalized rank,
top-1% enrichment,
top-5% enrichment,
top-10% enrichment.

All threshold analyses are secondary to continuous rank analysis.

============================================================
MULTIPLE TESTING
============================================================

Primary hypotheses will be distinguished from secondary/sensitivity
analyses.

False-discovery correction will be applied within prespecified
hypothesis families.

============================================================
MISSINGNESS
============================================================

Genes absent from a screen will be UNEVALUABLE, not unsupported.

No missing CRISPR score will be imputed.

============================================================
CROSS-SCREEN PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Influenza and RSV are analyzed independently first.

For RSV:
viability and early-infection screens constitute separate functional
phenotypes and will not initially be pooled.

Cross-screen convergence will be assessed only after individual-screen
results are frozen.
